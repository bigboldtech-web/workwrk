This page exists so you never open a WorkwrK surface expecting it to work and find out the hard way.

Three labels are used, and they mean exactly this:

- **Built**: you can use it end to end today. Verified in the code.
- **In progress**: the surface exists and part of it works. The specific gap is named. Do not rely on the missing half.
- **Coming soon**: the surface exists but there is no working backend behind it, or the backend exists with no way to reach it. Treat it as a placeholder.

Last verified against the codebase on 2026-08-14. Ordered by area.

## Settings

Settings is now a two-door nav. The **Admin** door (org configuration) is hidden entirely for anyone below Company Admin or Super Admin, and its most sensitive pages (`/settings/identity`, `/settings/tags`, `/settings/structure`, `/settings/audit`, `/settings/data`, `/settings/defaults`) also enforce a server-side redirect so a manager cannot reach them by URL. The **Personal** door (`/account/*` plus Notifications) is available to everyone.

## The legacy task table

Worth stating plainly because it explains several oddities above.

There are two task tables. Everything you create on a List is an `Item`. A separate legacy `Task` table still exists and is written only by `/api/tasks`.

Three places still read the legacy table and therefore cannot see your board work: `/analytics`, the performance score's task completion component, and `/api/tasks/workload`. Global search was a fourth until Wave 1 rewrote it over the `Item` graph. Migrating the rest is a tracked debt.
