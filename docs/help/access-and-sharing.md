# Access and sharing

Everyone in a workspace has one **workspace role**, and everything you make can be shared with people at one of **four levels**. That is the whole model.

## Workspace roles

| Role | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Runs the company account: billing, ownership and security. A workspace always has at least one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace day to day: opens every Space, invites people and uses every Workspace settings page. |
| **Member** | Works in the Spaces they are added to and sees what is open to the whole company. |
| **Guest** | Sees only what was shared with them. |

The **People team** is a group of Members (HR by default) who look after everyone's people information and can read Members, Structure, Access and Scoring in Workspace settings.

<Callout kind="info">
Only an Owner can make someone else an Owner, and the last Owner cannot be removed or demoted. A role change that takes something away signs the person out of their other devices on their next click.
</Callout>

## The four sharing levels

| Level | One sentence |
|---|---|
| **Full access** | Can do everything, including sharing it and deleting it. |
| **Can edit** | Can change it and add to it, but not share or delete it. |
| **Can comment** | Can read it and comment, but not change it. |
| **Can view** | Can read it. |

Sharing flows down: people on a Space reach its Folders and Lists. Adding someone never takes anything away. Marking a Folder or List **Private** stops new access from flowing into it from above; people who already had it keep it.

## Share something

1. Open the item's **...** menu, or the **Share** button in its header.
2. Type a name, pick a level, and choose **Share**.
3. **Who has access** lists everyone who can open it and why (they were added, they are on the Space, they are an Admin).

<Expandable title="Check what someone can do">
In the Share dialog, **Check access** answers, for one person, what they can do here and the one reason why. Use it before you change anything.
</Expandable>

## Ask for access

When a page says you cannot open something, choose **Request access**. The owner (or an Admin) sees the request under **Workspace settings > Access > Access requests** and can grant it at a level of their choice or decline it. You see **Request pending** on the button until they answer. Requests close on their own after 14 days.

## Public links

An Admin decides whether public links are allowed at all (**Workspace settings > Access > Public links**). Turning public links off turns every existing public link off too. Signing links and run links are separate and always work.

## Apps you do not see

An Admin can hide an app, or give it a minimum role, on **Workspace settings > Apps & modules**. Before the change is saved, the page says how many people lose access and names the first few. Nothing is deleted when an app is hidden, and turning it back on restores it for everyone.

<Callout kind="tip">
If an app has disappeared from your rail, ask one of the Admins named on the page you land on. They can see exactly who a change affects before they make it.
</Callout>
